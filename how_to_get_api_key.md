# How to Get Your Anthropic API Key
### (So the AI Tutor works in your 28-Day Program)

---

## What is this?

The Live AI Tutor inside the program needs a key to connect to Claude.
Think of it like a password that tells Anthropic "this person is allowed to use the AI."
It takes about 3 minutes to set up and costs nothing to start.

---

## Step 1 — Create a free account

Go to: **console.anthropic.com**

Click **Sign Up** and create an account with your email.
Verify your email when the confirmation arrives.

---

## Step 2 — Get your free credit

Anthropic gives new accounts **$5 of free credit** automatically.
Each conversation with the tutor costs roughly **$0.01–0.03**.
Your $5 will last the entire 28-day program and beyond.

---

## Step 3 — Create your API key

1. Once logged in, click **API Keys** in the left sidebar
2. Click **Create Key**
3. Give it a name — anything works, e.g. *"My AI Program"*
4. Click **Create**
5. **Copy the key immediately** — it starts with `sk-ant-...`
   ⚠️ You will not be able to see it again after closing this screen

---

## Step 4 — Paste it into the program

1. Open the 28-Day AI Mastery program
2. At the top you will see a purple bar: **🔑 Anthropic API Key**
3. Paste your key into the input field
4. Click **Connect**
5. The bar turns green — you are ready

The program remembers your key in this browser.
You will not need to paste it again on this device.

---

## Is it safe?

- Never share your key publicly or post it online
- You can delete and create a new key anytime at console.anthropic.com
- You can set a **spending limit** under *Billing → Usage Limits* — set it to $5 so you never get surprised

---

## Something not working?

Make sure your key starts with `sk-ant-` — if it looks different, you may have copied it incorrectly. Go back to console.anthropic.com, delete the old key, and create a fresh one.
